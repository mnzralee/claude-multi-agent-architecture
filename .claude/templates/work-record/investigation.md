# Investigation Template

Use this template to document the investigation and discovery process.

---

```markdown
### Investigation & Discovery

**Initial Hypothesis:**
[What I initially thought might be the cause/approach]

**Investigation Steps:**
1. [First thing checked] - [What I found]
2. [Second thing checked] - [What I found]
3. [Third thing checked] - [What I found]

**Dead Ends:**
[Optional - document approaches that didn't work]
- Tried [approach] - Didn't work because [reason]

**Key Discovery:**
[The "aha!" moment - what I realized that unlocked the solution]

**Root Cause:** (for bugs)
[Technical explanation of why the problem occurred]
```

---

## Guidelines

### Investigation Steps
- Document each step taken
- Include both successful and unsuccessful attempts
- Note what each step revealed
- Keep chronological order

### Dead Ends
- Don't skip these - they're valuable!
- Explain why the approach didn't work
- Future readers will benefit from knowing what NOT to try

### Key Discovery
- This is the narrative climax
- What was the insight that led to the solution?
- Make it clear and memorable

### Root Cause (for bugs)
- Go beyond symptoms to underlying cause
- Include code references when helpful
- Explain how this situation came to exist
